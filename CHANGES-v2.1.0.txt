v2.1.0

- Replaces the invalid one-stage MPAS init construction with CD-CT-compatible
  static and dynamic init stages.
- Generates case/static/mpas_init.yaml for one x1.<mesh>.static.nc product.
- Generates case/mpas_init.yaml only for date-dependent x1.<mesh>.init.nc products.
- Adds WPS_GEOG_ROOT, STATIC_NPROC, STATIC_QUEUE and STATIC_WALLTIME settings.
- Adds preflight checks for WPS_GEOG/topo_gmted2010_30s/index and for the
  static/dynamic namelist and stream contracts.
- Adds prepare-static, submit-static and validate-static commands.
- Makes all NMC commands require validation of the static product before
  dynamic initialization or forecast work.
